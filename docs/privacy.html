Who we are

Aaarto (https://aaarto.art) is operated by Goatstone (goatstone.com), based in the United States. To contact us, use the contact page.

What you draw stays in your browser until you mint

Your drawing is made in your browser. Nothing is sent to our server while you draw.

What is sent when you mint

When you press "Mint the Aaarto", the drawing (an SVG file), the title, the description and the artist name you typed are sent to our server, which uploads them to Pinata, a service that stores files on IPFS. IPFS is a public network: anyone can read what is stored there, and it cannot be reliably deleted. Do not put personal information in the title, description or artist name.

Our server does not keep the drawing or the text. It logs only errors and the reason an upload was rejected, and it counts requests per visitor (by IP address, in memory) to limit abuse. [Confirm: web server access logs, what they contain and how long they are kept.]

Your wallet and the blockchain

To mint, you connect a wallet. Your wallet address is used in your browser and in the mint transaction. We do not store it. The transaction, your wallet address, the token and the link to the artwork are recorded on the Polygon blockchain, which is public and permanent. Your wallet and the network providers it uses (for example RPC providers) see your IP address and your requests under their own policies.

What you send in the form (name, email, phone number and comments) is received through Google Forms and handled under Google's privacy policy. We use it only to answer you.
