Who we are

Aaarto (https://aaarto.art) is operated by an individual developer who resides in Peru (goatstone.com), not operated by a corporation or other business entity. To contact us, use the contact page or email aaarto_legal@goatstone.com.

What you draw stays in your browser until you mint

Your drawing is made in your browser. Nothing is sent to our server while you draw.

What is sent when you mint

When you press "Mint the Aaarto", the drawing (an SVG file), the title, the description and the artist name you typed are sent to our server, which uploads them to Pinata, a service that stores files on IPFS. IPFS is a public network: anyone can read what is stored there, and it cannot be reliably deleted. Do not put personal information in the title, description or artist name.

Our server does not keep the drawing, title, description or artist name after the upload is processed. It logs only errors and the reason an upload was rejected, and it counts requests per visitor (by IP address, in memory) to limit abuse. Our web server also keeps standard access logs (your IP address, the time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser) to run and secure the site. They are deleted after about two to three weeks. The web server's error log, which is rarely written to and can also contain an IP address, is kept longer, up to about two months.

Your wallet and the blockchain

To mint, you connect a wallet. Your wallet address is used in your browser and in the mint transaction. We do not maintain a separate database of wallet addresses. The transaction, your wallet address, the token and the link to the artwork are recorded on the Polygon blockchain, which is public and permanent, so your wallet address remains visible there. Your wallet and the network providers it uses (for example RPC providers) see your IP address and your requests under their own policies.

Cookies and tracking

Aaarto does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

The contact form

The contact page loads a Google Form from Google when the page opens, which lets Google set cookies. What you send in the form (name, email, phone number and comments) is received through Google Forms and handled under Google's privacy policy. We use it only to answer you. We keep messages for up to 12 months after our last reply, then delete them.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address and your message, use them only to answer you, and keep them for up to 12 months after our last reply, then delete them.

Other services

Pinata (file storage on IPFS), the RPC providers your wallet uses, the NFT marketplace you open (for example Rarible), and Google Forms each have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

Why we use personal information

We process personal information only as needed to operate the website, prevent abuse, respond to inquiries, upload content you choose to mint, and maintain the security of the service.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information under applicable privacy laws. These rights may include the right to request access to personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion of that information, object to or restrict certain processing, and receive information about how your data is used.

Because Aaarto stores very little personal information, and because information written to IPFS or recorded on a public blockchain cannot generally be modified or deleted, some rights may be limited in practice.

Residents of certain jurisdictions, including Peru, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, and other U.S. states with privacy laws, may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a privacy request, please use the contact page or email aaarto_legal@goatstone.com.

We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

Children's privacy

Aaarto is not directed at children, and you must be at least 18 to mint. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us at aaarto_legal@goatstone.com and we will delete it.
